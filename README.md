# 75 Hard Tracker

A beautiful, modern Progressive Web App to track your 75 Hard Challenge journey.

## Features

✨ **Daily Task Tracking**
- Follow your diet
- Complete two 45-minute workouts (one outdoor)
- Drink 1 gallon of water
- Read 10 pages of non-fiction
- Take daily progress photos

🛠️ **Custom Habits**
- Add your own habits alongside the 75 Hard defaults
- Edit or remove any habit
- Drag to reorder them in your daily list
- One-tap "Reset to 75 Hard Defaults"
- Progress photos are stored in IndexedDB (not localStorage), so the
  challenge never gets stuck on a day when storage fills up

📸 **Photo Progress**
- Before/after comparison slider
- Photo gallery with all your progress pics
- All photos stored locally on your device

📅 **Visual Calendar**
- See all 75 days at a glance
- Track completed vs incomplete days
- View your current streak

📊 **Stats Dashboard**
- Days completed
- Current streak
- Days remaining

🎨 **Beautiful Design**
- Modern glassmorphism UI
- Dark mode support (automatic)
- Smooth animations
- iOS-optimized interface

💾 **Offline First**
- All data stored locally on your phone
- No login required
- No internet needed (after first load)
- Never lose your progress!

## Installation on iPhone

1. Open Safari on your iPhone
2. Visit the app URL (provided after deployment)
3. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Tap "Add" in the top right
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen!

## How to Use

### Daily Routine
1. Check off each task as you complete it
2. Take your progress photo
3. Tap "Complete Day" when all tasks are done
4. Start fresh the next day!

### Viewing Progress
- Tap "Progress" tab to see your photo transformation
- Use the slider to compare Day 1 vs Today
- Browse your photo gallery

### Calendar View
- Tap "Calendar" to see all 75 days
- Green = completed days
- Red = incomplete days
- Orange = current day

### Settings
- View your start date
- Start a new challenge
- Clear all data (careful!)

## Important Notes

⚠️ **The 75 Hard Rules Are Strict**
- If you miss ANY task on any day, you must start over from Day 1
- The app will prompt you before restarting
- Your previous attempts are saved in history

💪 **Tips for Success**
- Set reminders on your phone
- Prepare your meals in advance
- Schedule your workouts
- Keep a book with you
- Take your photo at the same time each day

## Privacy

- **All data stays on YOUR phone**
- No servers, no accounts, no tracking
- Your photos are stored locally
- Complete privacy and security

## Technical Details

- Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Uses localStorage for data persistence
- Progressive Web App (PWA) technology
- Works offline after initial load
- Optimized for iOS Safari

## Support

If you encounter any issues:
1. Make sure you're using Safari on iOS
2. Check that you have enough storage space
3. Try clearing the app cache and reinstalling
